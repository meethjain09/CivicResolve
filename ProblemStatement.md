CivicResolve: AI-Powered Citizen Grievance Management and Resolution

Everyday civic issues such as potholes, overflowing garbage bins, damaged streetlights, water leakage, and poor road conditions can significantly affect communities. However, reporting these issues and ensuring they reach the appropriate authorities can often be a complicated and disconnected process.

Citizens may struggle to identify the right department, communicate the issue effectively, and track its resolution. At the same time, authorities need a structured way to understand, categorize, and prioritize incoming complaints.

The Challenge

Build CivicResolve, an AI-powered civic issue reporting and management platform that simplifies how citizens report local infrastructure and public-service issues.

Your application should allow citizens to report civic problems through a simple interface. Using AI capabilities powered by Google Gemini, the platform should intelligently understand the reported issue, categorize it, identify its urgency, and recommend the appropriate department for handling it.

Create a functional prototype that demonstrates how a reported civic issue can move from submission to tracking and resolution.

The goal is to make civic issue reporting more accessible, organized, and transparent through AI-powered assistance.

Expected Features
Citizen Issue Reporting: Allow users to submit civic complaints with a title, description, location, category, and optional image.
AI-Powered Classification: Use Google Gemini to analyze complaints and automatically identify the issue category.
Priority Detection: Classify issues based on urgency and potential public impact.
Department Recommendation: Suggest the appropriate department based on the reported issue.
Issue Tracking Dashboard: Display submitted complaints with their status, such as Reported, In Progress, and Resolved.
Admin Dashboard: Provide an interface for administrators to review complaints, update statuses, and manage reported issues.
Expected Outcome
A working web application that demonstrates an end-to-end civic issue reporting workflow, from citizen complaint submission to AI-assisted classification, prioritization, department recommendation, and status tracking.

Participants are encouraged to use Google Gemini and Google Antigravity creatively to build their solutions within the hackathon timeframe.

Build technology that helps communities report problems and visualize progress towards resolution.
